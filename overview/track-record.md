# Track Record Policy

Hydra Systems publishes performance only from independent, third-party
verified sources. It does not publish self-reported returns, screenshots of
balances, or selected winning periods.

## Why

Self-reported results are easy to cherry-pick and impossible for a reader to
check. A verified record, kept by a regulated platform or broker that the
reader can inspect directly, is the only result worth quoting.

## What Counts As Evidence

Hydra separates evidence into classes that are never added together:

| Class | What it shows | What it does not show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical backtest | How fixed rules would have behaved on past data under stated assumptions | Future results, real fills, or live behaviour |
| Forward shadow | Decisions made in real time without placing orders | Real execution or costs |
| Demo execution | Real order handling against a broker's demo environment | Real money outcomes |
| Verified live execution | Real trading with real money, verified by a third party | Future results |

The full rules, including the information every published result must carry,
are in the [Public Evidence Policy](../governance/public-evidence-policy.md).

## Where Results Will Appear

When a verified live record exists, it will be linked from this page and from
the dated [Public Operating Posture](../status/public-operating-posture.md).
Until then, no performance claim is made.

## Important Notice

Trading leveraged products carries a high risk of loss. Past or simulated
results do not indicate future results. Nothing here is investment advice or an
offer.
