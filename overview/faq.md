# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Hydra a trading bot I can buy or subscribe to?

No. Hydra Quant is a private trading platform. Its strategies are not sold,
licensed or shared. Hydra Guard, a separate risk-protection tool for
MetaTrader 5 traders, is in development; see the [Public Surface Registry](../architecture/public-surface-registry.md).

## Does Hydra make money?

Hydra Core makes no profitability claim. Results will only be published from
independent, verified sources. See the [track record policy](track-record.md).

## Can I invest in Hydra?

Not through this repository. Nothing here is an offer or investment advice.
If you are interested in the project, write to the address in the
[README](../README.md#enquiries).

## Why publish failures?

Because how a system fails says more about it than a list of winning trades.
The [risk-event ledger](../governance/risk-event-ledger.md) shows what went
wrong, what the controls did, and what changed afterwards, with private detail
removed.

## What is the difference between Hydra Guardian and Hydra Guard?

Hydra Guardian is the internal supervisory and risk authority inside Hydra
Quant; it is not sold. Hydra Guard is a separate product for MetaTrader 5
traders that helps them stay inside their own risk rules.

## Why are the strategies secret?

A strategy's edge tends to disappear once it is widely copied. The controls
around the strategies, which matter just as much, are documented here in full.

## Who builds Hydra?

Hydra is founded and run by Ibz, an independent systematic trader and engineer
in the UK, working with AI engineering assistants under human review. Every
live change needs a written, approved procedure.

## I'm an engineer. Can I review or contribute?

Yes. Start with the [System Overview](../architecture/system-overview.md) and
the [Contributing Guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md), or get in touch through the
[README](../README.md#enquiries).
