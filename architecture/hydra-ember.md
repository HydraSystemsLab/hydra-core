# Hydra Ember

Hydra Ember is a private market scanner and research pipeline governed by Hydra Core doctrine.

It is a research surface.
It is not public execution authority.

## Purpose

Hydra Ember exists to support early-signal research without blurring the boundary between research output and governed action.

The purpose is to make research reviewable, replayable, and accountable before it can influence any promoted operating posture.

## What It Is

Hydra Ember focuses on:

- offline data ingestion
- early-signal research
- replay and walk-forward checks
- dashboards
- review packs

These outputs help operators and researchers inspect whether a signal class deserves more work.
They do not make the system live.

## Operating Posture

Hydra Ember is research-first.

Scanner output must remain separated from execution authority.
Promotion requires evidence, review, and controls.

## What Is Not Public

Hydra Ember does not publish private filter logic, source data, data access arrangements, or scanner internals.

The public docs describe how the surface is bounded.
They do not publish the research machinery.

## Relationship To Core

Hydra Core defines the doctrine Ember must follow:

- survivability over activity
- evidence before promotion
- research is not live authority
- ambiguous state should narrow permission, not expand it
- operator review remains part of promotion
